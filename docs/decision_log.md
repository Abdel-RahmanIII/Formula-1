Requiring zero laps keeps everyone who took the start, including opening-lap retirements from a grid slot. Removes 1,715 rows (1,367 `F`, 331 `W`, 17 excluded / disqualified / did-not-start entries with no grid slot) and no podium finisher. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-010** | 2026-10-12 | Scope / Cleaning | M1 | Pit-lane starts: after DEC-009, every remaining `grid == 0` row gets `pit_start = 1` and `grid` recoded to the last occupied grid slot of that race + 1. | After DEC-009 the remaining `grid == 0` rows are 72 entries from 2015–2024, all with racing laps completed: genuine pit-lane starts. Left as `0`, an ordinal model would read them as better than pole. Pit-lane starters in the same race tie on the recoded value; `pit_start` keeps them distinguishable. None of the 72 finished on the podium. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-011** | 2026-10-12 | DE Questions | M2 | Q1 compares 2003–2013 with 2014–2024, for both the grid-based and the qualifying-based front row. A grid fallback for missing qualifying was rejected: it would force the qualifying-vs-grid gap to zero. The filter applies inside Q1 only; `clean_df` is unchanged. 14 circuits pass the threshold under either definition. | **Approved** |
| **DEC-012** | 2026-10-12 | DE Questions | M2 | Q2 home race = `circuits.country` is one of the countries mapped from `drivers.nationality` (`NATIONALITY_TO_COUNTRIES`). | Country names follow `circuits.country` (`British` → `UK`, `American` → `USA`); `United States` is folded into `USA`. `Monegasque` → `Monaco`, `East German` → `Germany`. | **DEC-013** | 2026-10-12 | DE Questions | M2 | Q3 status mapping to *finished / mechanical / accident-driver / regulatory-other*, with a strict rule for mechanical: the label must name a failed car component or system. The grouping is a judgment call that is not in the data, which is why it is a check and not the main answer. | **Approved** |
| **DEC-015** | 2026-10-12 | Feature Engineering | M3 | Every points feature is a share, not a raw total: `constructor_rolling_points_5` is the constructor's share of all points awarded in its last 5 races; `driver_pre_race_points` and `constructor_pre_race_points` are a share of the championship leader's points. | A win was worth 8–9 points until 1990, 10 from 1991 and 25 from 2010. The training seasons ($\le 2019$) are mostly on the old scales while validation and test are entirely on the new one, so raw points would shift meaning between the splits. A share is on the same 0–1 scale in every season. The feature names reserved in `FEATURE_GROUPS` are kept. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-016** | 2026-10-12 | Feature Engineering | M3 | Sprint features are left out; the `sprint` group is removed from `FEATURE_GROUPS`. | The first sprint was held in 2021. The training seasons contain 0 rows with a sprint result (validation 57, test 283), so a model cannot learn anything from the column. The plan lists sprint as optional. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-017** | 2026-10-12 | Feature Engineering | M3 | Qualifying features stay `NaN` where the source has no qualifying record, with `missing_quali_flag = 1`. `gap_to_pole` is the best of Q1–Q3 as % slower than the fastest qualifier; a gap above 100% is set to `NaN`. `q3_reached` is defined only where the race had a Q3 session. | `quali_position` is missing in 63.1% of training rows but 0.1% of validation and 0% of test rows. Filling it (for example from `grid`) is a preprocessing choice that must be fitted on train only, so it is deferred to Step 5 and its window experiment. The 100% rule removes one recording error (1995 Japanese GP, `q1` = 1,002.64 s, a 923% gap) and keeps genuine wet-session gaps of 30–45%. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-018** | 2026-10-12 | Feature Engineering | M3 | `circuit_historical_podium_rate` is the driver's own podium rate in earlier races at this circuit. The rolling window for form features is 5 races. | Across all drivers, a circuit's podium rate is just 3 divided by the field size, which `field_size` already carries. The driver-at-circuit version has signal (ROC-AUC 0.67 alone on the training seasons). Window: a 10-race window scores higher alone (0.834 vs. 0.812), but together with the career rate the driver-form group reaches 0.843 with 5 races and 0.846 with 10, so the shorter, more responsive window is kept. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-019** | 2026-10-12 | Split / Preprocessing | M3 | Train on all seasons up to 2019 (no minimum year). A missing `quali_position` is filled with the row's grid slot. | Window experiment on validation PR-AUC (2020–2021), logistic regression: all seasons 0.729, from 1994 0.719, from 2003 0.725, from 2006 0.728; all within about 0.01, inside the bootstrap noise of 39 validation races. Median vs. grid fallback: 0.7290 vs. 0.7284. Gradient boosting on raw features falls from 0.704 (all seasons) to 0.665 (from 2006). Deleting the seasons without qualifying gains nothing for one model and hurts the other, so no rows are dropped. The grid fallback is kept because qualifying and grid agree in 98% of 1994–2002 rows that have both. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-020** | 2026-10-12 | Split / Preprocessing | M3 | Preprocessing pipeline: grid fallback for `quali_position`, median imputation and standardisation for the 16 continuous features, missing binary flags filled with 0 and left unscaled. No encoder. | All 21 features are numeric or 0/1 flags. `driverId` and `constructorId` are not one-hot encoded: 13.7% of validation rows and 21.9% of test rows belong to a driver never seen in training (15.6% and 20.0% for constructors), so their one-hot columns would be all zeros. Form, standings and circuit-history features describe the same thing and also work for newcomers. | **Proposed** |
| **DEC-021** | 2026-10-12 | Split / Preprocessing | M3 | The preprocessor is fitted on training rows only, in the order impute → scale. | Experiment 1 (fit on train vs. on all rows): validation PR-AUC Δ = 0.0000 (95% interval −0.0005 to +0.0003); the extra rows are 8.7% of the data and move the learned standard deviations by 1–2%. Experiment 2 (impute → scale vs. scale → impute): Δ = −0.0001 (−0.0006 to +0.0001), although imputing first shrinks the standard deviation learned for `gap_to_pole` from 2.57 to 1.57. Neither changes the score here; train-only fitting is kept because it is free and correct by construction, and impute → scale because it guarantees mean 0 and std 1 on the training columns. | **Proposed** |
